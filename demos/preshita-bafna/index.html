For Preshita · the idea

Your team already asks the right questions. Let brides answer them before they message.

Today, a bride asks “what's the price?” in your DMs. Your team replies with a form (name, phone, date, venue/city, number of looks), she types it all out, then the quote comes later. Here's the same process, done before she even messages you.
