About Alice

Italian roots, British weddings.

Alice grew up surrounded by Milanese glamour and fashion, and fell in love with hair styling there. Bridal Hairstyles began during the pandemic, around the time she became a mum. Questions brides ask

Good to know

Do I need a trial?

Yes, if you can. Your trial is at Alice's studio in Wilford, usually six to ten weeks before the wedding. Bring your inspiration photos, your veil or accessories and, ideally, a photo of your dress. Can't make a trial? Alice has styled brides beautifully on the day from photos alone.

Can Alice do my bridesmaids and mum as well?

Yes. Each person can have a different style, and Alice plans the morning so everyone is finished in good time and you are never rushed.

How far ahead should I book?

As soon as your date is set. Summer Saturdays go first, and Alice can only be at one wedding a day. The 2027 diary is open now.

What if Alice is already booked on my date?

Join the waiting list for that date. If it frees up, you'll get a message straight away and first refusal.

Do you only do weddings?

Weddings are the heart of it, but Alice also does hair for special occasions. Message her with the date and what you have in mind.
