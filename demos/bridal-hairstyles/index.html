Straight answers

Things you'd want to know before saying yes.

Do I need a new website?

No. Your website was designed for you and it's beautiful. The booking engine replaces your Check Availability form and links from your Book Now buttons. Everything else stays exactly as it is.

Will you take a cut of my bookings?

No. There's no commission on any booking, ever. Deposits are paid straight into your own account (Stripe), not through me.

What will it cost?

I'll give you one clear price in writing before anything is built, so there are no surprises. If it isn't worth it to you, you simply say no.

Can I still be personal with my brides?

Yes, that's the point. You approve the wording of every message. Brides can still WhatsApp you, and you still confirm their schedule, now with all their details in front of you.

What about large bridal parties?

The engine works out the morning from her access and ceremony times. If the party is too big for the time, it tells the bride you'll arrange a second stylist and flags it for you, just as your FAQ says.

Who owns the brides' details?

You do. Bookings, contacts and e-book subscribers are yours, exportable at any time and kept to UK GDPR standards.

What in this demo is real?

Your prices, deposit, process, form fields, studio hours, rating and photos come from your website and Google listing. The booked dates and the brides in the diary are made up for the demo, and no card is ever charged here.

What if I try it and don't like it?

Then your old form goes back on. Nothing changes on your website until you've seen it working and said yes.
