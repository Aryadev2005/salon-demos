1 · What brides see

Her own booking link, no website needed.

Each student puts one link in her Instagram bio, stories and TikTok. Brides send a proper enquiry in two minutes, with everything a stylist needs to know.

  • Asks the questions you teach students to ask
  • Shows an instant estimate from the student's own prices
  • Dates she's already booked show as taken
  • “Trained by Alice Paonessa” builds trust for a new stylist, and brings your name to every bride she meets
Try it: fill it in and send. Then tap 2 · Student's app to see it arrive.

2 · What your student sees

Your method, in her pocket.

Every bride moves through your steps, from enquiry to wedding day. At each step, your student sees your tip and a message written in your words, ready to send on WhatsApp in one tap.

  • Always shows what to do next, for every bride
  • Payments simply ticked off: deposit, trial, balance
  • Trial notes saved as her “recipe” for the day
  • Wedding-morning schedule worked out from the access and ceremony times
  • Your kit list, ready the night before
Try it: tap a bride, send the next message, tick a payment, and move her to the next step.
