Good to know

Quick answers.

Where are Beyond Beauty Salon's branches?

Beyond has three branches in Egypt: The Lane Mall in Palm Hills, Sheikh Zayed; 9 El Gezira El Wosta Street in Zamalek, Cairo; and a summer branch in Sahel on the North Coast.

How do I book an appointment at Beyond?

Book right here on the website: choose your branch, services, stylist, date and time, and you get a booking reference instantly. No app, no account and no deposit.

Does Beyond do bridal hair?

Yes. Beyond styles brides for engagements, katb ketab, henna and weddings, including veil placement, with a trial session before the day. Bridal consultations can be booked at any branch.

Who founded Beyond Beauty Salon?

Beyond was founded by Ahmed Batekha, a professional hairstylist known for glamour styling, hair colour and bridal hair.

Does Beyond do nails as well as hair?

Yes. Beyond offers gel sets, nail extensions, manicures and pedicures, so you can book hair and nails in the same visit.
