Your questions

Straight answers, before any call.

What is the one-time setup cost?

€399, and you only pay it once the whole system is live on your website. That covers your booking page with all your services and prices, WhatsApp confirmations and reminders, deposits, gift vouchers, your reviews on the site, and connecting it all to your website.

Are there any other fees or booking commissions?

For the first 4 weeks after go-live it's €100 a week, while you watch the results live on your dashboard. After that you pay nothing until the system has brought you €1,000 in extra bookings. Once it has, we sit down and agree the ongoing price together, based on what it actually made you. No commission on any booking, ever.

How do you count the €1,000?

Only bookings the system creates count: cancelled slots refilled from your waiting list, quiet times filled by a WhatsApp offer, and clients who rebook after a reminder. Your normal bookings don't count. Every one is tagged on your dashboard, so you see the total grow.

Can it be integrated with my existing website?

Yes. Your website stays exactly as it is. We point your "Maak een afspraak" buttons to your booking page, or show the booking right inside your page. We can do it ourselves or send Giling Media a short code snippet.

Will I have full ownership and control of the system and my client data?

Yes. Your clients, bookings and vouchers belong to you. You can export everything as a spreadsheet at any time, and if you ever stop, you take it all with you. Data is stored in the EU, under the AVG (GDPR).

How much could I save compared with Treatwell?

It depends on how many new clients come through the Treatwell app. Use the calculator above with your own numbers. As a rough example, one new €90 colour client through the marketplace costs about €38 in commission.

What in this demo is real?

Your services, prices, opening hours, address and reviews come from your website and Treatwell page. Treatment durations are my estimates (you set your own), and the clients and bookings in the diary are made up. No card is charged here.
