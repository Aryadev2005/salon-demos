For Preeti · the idea

Every booking happens on WhatsApp today. Let brides arrive with everything you need.

Today, every booking is done by hand on WhatsApp: the date, the city, the functions, the family looks, the quote, the advance, the trial. Here's the same process, with the bride doing the first half herself, before she even messages you.

Today

  1. Bride DMs “price for bridal makeup?”Often while she's messaging five other artists
  2. You reply asking for her date, city and functionsMeanwhile she's waiting, and still messaging other artists
  3. She replies bit by bit, between other chats
  4. You check your diary, work out the quote, explain what's included
  5. Advance, trial and events are agreed over many messages

With your booking link

  1. She taps the link in your bio, at any hourSees straight away whether her date is free
  2. She picks her functions and looksHaldi, mehendi, sangeet, wedding, reception
  3. She sees a starting estimateOr “quote on request”, as you prefer
  4. Her complete details land on your WhatsAppAnd she has already seen what's included, so no confusion about extras
