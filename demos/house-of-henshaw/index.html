Our story

A neighbourhood salon where the team actually listens.

Kate Henshaw built House of Henshaw around one idea: exceptional hairdressing in a warm, friendly, stylish space. Bring a picture and we'll follow it, or tell you honestly if it won't suit. We never overbook, so there's no waiting around, and the kettle is always on.

We work with MY.ORGANICS, Davroe and Act+Acre, and we're training the next generation of stylists with Andrew Collinge.

Good to know

Quick answers.

Where is House of Henshaw?

At 303 Aigburth Road, Liverpool L17 0BJ, in Aigburth, South Liverpool. The salon is on the first floor.

What are your opening hours?

Monday 10am to 4pm, Tuesday to Thursday 9am to 8pm, Friday 9am to 5pm, Saturday 8am to 4pm. Closed on Sunday.

What is Serenity?

Our science-led scalp and wellness studio upstairs: headspa treatments, Dermalux LED light therapy, root cause hair testing from £140, and massage, for hair loss, thinning and hormonal changes.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes. Hair and colour vouchers come in £100, £200 and £300, and Serenity scalp and wellness vouchers start from £20.

Can I bring a picture of the look I want?

Please do. We'll follow it closely, and if it won't suit your hair we'll tell you honestly and suggest the closest look that will.

Have you won any awards?

Yes: North West Salon of the Year 2014, and North West Junior Stylist of the Year in 2017 and 2019, at the English Hair and Beauty Awards.
