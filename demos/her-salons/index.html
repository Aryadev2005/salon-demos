Good to know

Before your visit.

I'm new. What should I prepare?

Send the salon a current photo of your hair and a few words about what you'd like to achieve. If you're unsure what to book, ask and the team will recommend the right services.

Can I book a consultation first?

Yes, and we strongly recommend it. A consultation is $50, and it's deducted from your next booking.

Is a trim the same price as a haircut?

Yes, every haircut length is the same price. If you need extra time for a restyle or very thick hair, choose the restyle option. A blow-dry is included with every haircut.

What's the cancellation policy?

Reschedule up to 48 hours before and your deposit moves with you. Cancellations within 48 hours are charged 30%, and within 24 hours or no-shows are charged in full.

Where do I park in Perth?

For about an hour, street parking on Oxford Street and around Mount Hawthorn. For two to three hours, The Mezz, 148 Scarborough Beach Road.
