Showing up in ChatGPT and Google's AI answers

More people now ask an AI assistant for a clinic instead of scrolling a map. These assistants have no list of their own. They read what is already public about you and name the clinics they can describe with confidence. What gets a clinic named:

  • Details a machine can readStructured data on the site for the clinic, the practitioner, each treatment, hours and reviews, so an assistant doesn't have to guess.
